Aangan Living demo website

1. Unzip this folder (right click > Extract All). Do not open files from inside the zip.
2. Double click index.html to open the website in Chrome or Edge.
3. Owner dashboard: owner-dashboard.html (or click 'Owner login' on the website).
4. Ad previews: marketing/presentation-ad.html (click to replay).

Needs an internet connection for fonts, icons and charts.
All names and numbers are sample data for the demo.
