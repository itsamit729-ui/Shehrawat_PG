FAQ

Questions people ask before booking

Is there any brokerage or hidden charge?

No brokerage. You pay monthly rent, a refundable security deposit equal to one month of rent and your sub metered electricity. The cost calculator above shows the full amount.

How much is the booking token?

₹2,000, adjusted in your first month's rent. It is fully refundable if you cancel at least 72 hours before your move in date.

What is the notice period?

30 days for monthly plans and 15 days for stays under 3 months. You can give notice from the resident app.

When will I get my deposit back?

Within 7 working days after move out, minus any damage that was recorded on the move in checklist. We add ₹100 for every day we are late.

Can I visit before booking?

Yes. Book an in person visit or a live video tour, every day from 10 AM to 8 PM.

Are guests allowed?

Day guests are welcome in common areas till 9 PM. Overnight guest stays are available at ₹699 a night in select homes, subject to availability.

Is food vegetarian?

All homes serve vegetarian meals daily with non veg on Wednesday and Sunday. Jain and egg options are available on request.

Can I change my room later?

Yes. Switch between single, double and triple or move to another Aangan home. Your deposit carries over.
